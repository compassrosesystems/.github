# Contributing

- **Plane is the source of truth for planning.** Every PR references a Plane work item (`LL-42`) in its title or body.
- **Branches:** `feat/LL-42-short-name`, `fix/LL-42-short-name`.
- **No direct pushes to `main`.** Open a PR and get one review from someone else on the team.
- **Secrets never go in the repo.** That includes `.env` files. Use the team vault.
- **Copy:** EN by default, pt-PT (Portugal, never Brazil) where Portuguese is needed.
